Our Legacy

More Than Fifty Years of Fire Protection Engineering

Established in 1975, New Fire Engineers Pvt. Ltd. stands as one of the foremost Fire Protection Engineering Companies in India, certified by ISO 9001:2015. With more than fifty years of experience, New Fire offers fully customized turnkey solutions in fire detection, fire suppression, and fire protection systems tailored for industry needs.

Our services encompass a wide range of sectors, including Offshore, Marine, and Commercial Business Sectors both in India and internationally, extending to regions such as Africa and the Middle East. Our clientele includes prominent Power Plants, Oil Refineries, Petrochemical facilities, Factories, Buildings, Public Sector Undertakings, Defence, and Infrastructure Businesses.

At New Fire, we are a committed team equipped with sufficient manpower resources, led by a group of professionally qualified and experienced engineers in this domain. They possess the capability to design, procure, and execute large-scale projects from the initial concept through to the commissioning stages. New Fire stands unmatched in its role as a comprehensive solution provider across the region.

New Fire is motivated by its commitment to enhancing the welfare and development of the nation by mitigating the loss of life and property, while simultaneously improving business continuity through the use of emerging technologies and the implementation of environmentally friendly extinguishing methods. For example, New Fire took the initiative to advocate for the Halon Alternate Inert Gas system a decade ago, recognizing the Global Warming Effects of HFC. Consequently, both Inert gas and Water Mist were vigorously promoted as environmentally friendly alternatives to Halon.

Our Zeal & Mission

Enhancing welfare and development by mitigating loss of life and property, improving business continuity, and deploying emerging, environmentally friendly extinguishing methods.
